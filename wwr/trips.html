Our Featured Trips

Devil's Teeth Rapids

One of our most popular routes, Devil's Teeth offers a perfect combination of exciting rapids and stunning scenery. This full-day trip includes riverside lunch and swimming stops in calm waters.

Ideal for groups of friends and families with teenagers. Our certified guides will accompany you every step, ensuring a safe and fun experience.

Class III-IV Difficulty
7 hours Duration
12+ years Minimum age
$125 Per person

Big Mallard Adventure

Designed for beginners and families, Big Mallard offers a gentle introduction to the world of rafting. Enjoy moderate rapids, calm swimming waters, and spectacular scenery along the way.

This trip includes a complete instructional session before starting, ensuring even beginners feel comfortable and safe on the water.

Class I-II Difficulty
4 hours Duration
8+ years Minimum age
$85 Per person

Extreme Rapids Expedition

For experienced adventurers seeking the ultimate challenge, our Extreme Rapids Expedition will take you through the most technical and exciting rapids in the region. This two-day trip includes rustic riverside camping.

Requires previous rafting experience and good physical condition. All participants receive specialized equipment and advanced training before starting.

Class IV-V Difficulty
2 days Duration
18+ years Minimum age
$350 Per person
